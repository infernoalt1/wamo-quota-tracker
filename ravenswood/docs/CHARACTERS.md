# Trouble Brewing character research

Research date: 2 November 2026. Scope: Trouble Brewing's 22 characters, no Travellers, Fabled, or other editions. The descriptions below are paraphrases of the official character almanac. The linked sources, not this implementation summary, are authoritative. Each role summary is intentionally concise. The full game rules and night order also need to be respected.

## Townsfolk

| Character and official source | Trigger and implementation constraints |
| --- | --- |
| [Washerwoman](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Washerwoman) | First night only: show two distinct players and a Townsfolk character. One must register as that character; the other is a decoy. Choose helpful or ambiguous pairings, but a healthy Washerwoman cannot receive arbitrary misinformation. A healthy Spy may register as a particular Townsfolk, including one not actually in play. A Drunk does not register as their believed Townsfolk. |
| [Librarian](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Librarian) | First night only: show two players and an Outsider character, or report zero Outsiders. Drunk is the actual character to reveal, without revealing their believed Townsfolk. Spy registration can support an Outsider result. Use the actual or legally registered character, not the identity shown to the target. |
| [Investigator](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Investigator) | First night only: show two players and a Minion character. One must register as that Minion. Recluse can register as a particular Minion, allowing a pairing containing no actual evil player. Without poisoning, drunkenness or legal misregistration, the information must be correct. |
| [Chef](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Chef) | First night: count adjacent evil pairs in the seating circle, including the last and first seats. Three consecutive evil players yield two pairs. Misregistration can be chosen separately for each pair: the official example allows Recluse between two evil players to count as evil on one edge and good on the other. Do not require a single globally consistent alignment for the entire calculation. |
| [Empath](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Empath) | Every night: count evil among the closest living player in each direction. Resolve after Demon deaths; skip all dead seats, including newly dead neighbors. Information is 0, 1 or 2. Spy/Recluse registration can change the result. A dead Empath does not receive a further result. |
| [Fortune Teller](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Fortune_Teller) | Every night: choose two distinct players, including self or dead players. Return yes when either registers as Demon or is the red herring. Choose the red herring from good players at setup, possibly Fortune Teller themselves; never move it. Dead Imp and dead red herring still give yes. Recluse can register Demon. Poisoning a real Imp does not stop it registering as Imp. |
| [Undertaker](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Undertaker) | Nights after the first: reveal character of the player who died by execution today. If nobody died by execution, give no character result. Virgin execution counts; Slayer death does not. Executing an already-dead person causes no death and no result. Drunk reveals Drunk; healthy Spy/Recluse can misregister despite being dead. |
| [Monk](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Monk) | Nights after the first: choose anyone except self, including dead players. Functioning protection prevents the Demon's ability harming that player that night. No replacement attack occurs. Protected Mayor does not redirect. A protected Imp targeting itself cannot die or starpass. Protection ends at dawn or immediately if its source loses the ability. |
| [Ravenkeeper](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Ravenkeeper) | On dying at night, choose any player that same night, including dead players or self, then learn their character. Mayor redirect can cause the triggering death. Daytime execution or Slayer death cannot. Spy/Recluse can misregister; Drunk ordinarily reveals actual Drunk. A Drunk who believes they are Ravenkeeper still receives a simulated wake and information following night death. |
| [Virgin](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Virgin) | First valid nomination while alive: a functioning Virgin immediately executes a nominator registering as Townsfolk. End nominations and proceed toward night, replacing any previous execution candidate. First nomination consumes the ability even when the nominator is not Townsfolk or Virgin is impaired. Self-nomination can trigger. Spy may register Townsfolk and die; Drunk cannot. Invalid nomination by a dead player does not consume the ability. |
| [Slayer](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Slayer) | Once during the game, publicly select someone during day. A functioning shot kills a living Demon, or a healthy Recluse chosen to register Demon. A shot is spent even if impaired or aimed at a dead player. Let every player make bluff shots with the same public presentation; never confirm the shooter's actual character. Check Scarlet Woman succession before checking Demon-death victory. |
| [Soldier](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Soldier) | Functioning passive protection from the Demon's ability. Imp attack fails without another attack; a Mayor bounce onto Soldier also fails. Execution still kills. Poisoning and the Drunk's absent ability remove protection. Do not announce the target was attacked or the reason no one died. |
| [Mayor](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Mayor) | At dusk, a living, functioning Mayor wins for good if exactly three players live and no execution occurred that day. If Mayor would die at night, Storyteller may allow it or redirect to another player. Redirecting the Demon attack to a dead player, functioning Soldier or Monk-protected player gives no death. The wiki recommends usually preserving Mayor to the final day, occasionally allowing death when their early confirmation makes evil's position too weak. |

## Outsiders

| Character and official source | Trigger and implementation constraints |
| --- | --- |
| [Butler](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Butler) | Each night choose someone except self as master, including a dead player or the previous master. Next day may vote only while master is voting or after master's vote counted. Never forced to vote. Dead Butler loses the restriction and may freely spend their remaining vote. The wiki says to count accidental illegal votes, then privately correct the player, rather than reveal Butler through a missing vote. Use private reminders rather than exposing master or identity. |
| [Drunk](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Drunk) | Actual Outsider, secretly shown an unused Townsfolk identity. Simulate that Townsfolk's prompts and information, but never its mechanical effects. Information can be true or false. Store actual and shown character separately, exposing only shown identity to the player. Poisoning Drunk does not restore a Townsfolk ability. Other functioning abilities detect Drunk as Drunk. |
| [Recluse](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Recluse) | Actually good Outsider. While healthy may register evil, Minion or Demon, even dead. Choices may differ among abilities and interactions. Never gains the registered character's abilities. Can give Investigator a Minion pairing, Fortune Teller a positive result, altered Chef/Empath information, or an evil character result for Undertaker/Ravenkeeper; may die to Slayer. Poisoning suppresses misregistration. |
| [Saint](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Saint) | A functioning Saint dying by execution immediately loses for good. Other causes of death do not trigger. A poisoned Saint can die by execution without this loss. Executing an already-dead Saint does not cause another death or trigger the loss. |

## Evil characters

| Character and official source | Trigger and implementation constraints |
| --- | --- |
| [Poisoner](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Poisoner) | Each night select any player to poison that night and next day. Wake poisoned players normally, but their abilities have no effect and information may be false. Poison ends at dusk, or immediately if Poisoner dies or changes character. A poisoned once-per-game attempt is still spent. Poisoning a Saint can allow safe execution; poisoning a Soldier enables the Demon to kill them. |
| [Spy](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Spy) | Each night while alive inspect the Grimoire, including actual characters and statuses. While healthy may register good, Townsfolk or Outsider, including when dead; choices can vary by interaction. Does not acquire the registered role's ability. Can activate Virgin as a registered Townsfolk and later appear as an Outsider to Undertaker. Death preserves registration, not nightly Grimoire viewing. Poisoning suppresses registration and permits false Grimoire information. |
| [Scarlet Woman](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Scarlet_Woman) | While alive and functioning, becomes the Demon when Demon dies if at least five players were alive immediately before that death, counting the dying Demon. Resolve succession before checking victory. Can trigger on execution, Slayer death or an Imp killing itself. Does not preserve evil with only four alive before the death. |
| [Baron](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Baron) | At setup replace two Townsfolk with two additional Outsiders. This affects actual role counts, including Drunk as an Outsider, not displayed character counts. The completed setup is not undone when Baron dies, changes role or is poisoned. |
| [Imp](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Imp) | Each night after the first chooses any player to kill, including self or dead players. A functioning self-kill makes a living Minion into Imp; the Storyteller selects the recipient subject to Scarlet Woman's ability. Protection or poisoning that prevents self-death prevents succession. A former Imp remains a dead Demon for character detection. |

## Shared rules that the engine must enforce

- [Abilities](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Abilities): resolve immediately. Death prevents later night actions except explicit death-triggered abilities. Persistent effects end when their source dies or loses/changes ability. Only explicitly retained dead-character abilities continue. Public announcements reveal deaths, not causes or characters; protection is not disclosed to its recipient.
- [States](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/States): execution and death are different events. A dead player can be executed, consuming the daily execution without dying again. Drunk/poisoned information may be true or false, but mechanical abilities do not work. Always answer rules questions truthfully. Impairment of the subject does not generally impair another character's detection ability.
- [Storyteller Advice](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Storyteller_Advice): continue while either team retains any possible path to victory. Do not declare evil's win prematurely if protection might prevent the next night death. Preserve bluffing through consistent presentation of public actions and keep private questions private.

## Rules-constrained balancing

The automation should generate legal outcomes first, then choose among them. It can use plausible misinformation for impaired information roles, Spy/Recluse registration, setup pairings, fixed red-herring selection, and Mayor redirects to keep competing explanations viable. Helping the weaker team never authorizes moving the red herring, inventing poison, changing player choices, falsifying ordinary healthy information, changing votes or undoing valid deaths.

Decision records should identify the ability, legal basis, available candidates and reason. Keep those records secret during play; reveal them after game end for review. A heuristic engine should describe itself as a rules-based automatic Storyteller rather than claiming it semantically understands the discussion.
