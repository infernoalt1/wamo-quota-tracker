How points work

Guessers: order + speed
100-500 points based on time remaining, plus 400 divided by your finishing position: first +400, second +200, third +133. Whole-point rounding.

Artist: up to 1,200 points
1,000 times the fraction who solve, plus up to 200 for how quickly they solve.

Whole-point scores are calculated only after the drawing ends. Hints reveal a letter at 1/3 and 2/3 of the timer. Mid-turn arrivals play from the next drawing.
