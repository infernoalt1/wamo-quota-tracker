FIELD MANUAL / 01

Say enough.
Don’t say too much.

4–8 players. Two teams. Four secret words per team, fixed for the whole game.

  1. Encrypt. Your team’s rotating encryptor gets a secret three-digit code, such as 3 · 1 · 4. Write one clue for each corresponding word, in that order.
  2. Decode. Discuss with your team and lock in three different digits from 1–4. The encryptor stays silent until their code is revealed.
  3. Intercept. Starting in round two, guess the other team’s code using their past clues. Both guesses lock before the code is revealed.
  4. Win. Earn two interceptions, or let your opponents make two miscommunications. Both teams play before scores decide the round.

Clues must refer to word meanings, never spelling, position, pronunciation, private references, the keywords themselves, or their translations. Never repeat a clue. Players judge meaning-based rules together.

Both encryptors write first; Blue’s transmission resolves before Amber’s. After eight rounds or simultaneous win/loss conditions, compare interceptions minus miscommunications. Still tied? Guess all four opposing words in order. Exact matches count; another tie is a shared win.

This online version is untimed. One teammate submits the team’s agreed answer. Team switching and spectating are available in the lobby. The host can return everyone to the lobby to regroup; the next game deals fresh words.
