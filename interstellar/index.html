01 / ORBITAL MOTION

Everything has a little pull.

Gravity holds our solar system together. Planets travel around the Sun, while moons orbit planets. These paths are not perfect circles: they are ellipses, with the Sun at one focus of each planet’s orbit.

02 / STELLAR SCIENCE

Small lights. Extraordinary stories.

Stars are enormous spheres of hot gas. Deep inside, nuclear fusion releases the energy that makes them shine. A star’s mass helps determine its temperature, brightness, and how long it lives.

The Sun is our nearest star. Studying it helps us understand the more distant stars scattered across the night sky.
