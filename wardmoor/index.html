Wardmoor

Nine regions, one moor. Take the standing stones, earn renown, and spend your nights worth hunting.

Needs a keyboard and mouse. Press Esc in the game for the rules.

How to play

Controls

  • WASD or arrows move.
  • Left mouse uses what you hold. Hold it to keep attacking or keep your buckler up.
  • 1 to 7 change tools.
  • Q changes armor, but not within 8 seconds of being hit.

Renown

Monsters and rivals pay renown, which unlocks tools and armor. A rival is worth 12 plus a quarter of their renown, so leaders are valuable targets. When you die you lose 40% of yours, and anything you can no longer afford locks again.

Standing stones

Stay inside a stone's ring with no rival inside to claim its region. Bring friends to claim faster. Owning a region pays 25% more renown for kills there and raises militia who guard it. The middle of the moor has the strongest monsters.

Warbands

Enter the same warband name as your friends. You can't hurt each other, and regions belong to the whole band.
