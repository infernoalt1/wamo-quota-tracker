Fighting

You fight with the tools in your toolbelt: weapons, shields, maneuvers, heals, thrown concoctions, traps and banners. Every tool has a wind-up, a timed sequence and a cooldown. Every tool has its own cooldown. An attack keeps you busy while it plays out, so you can weave a second weapon or a sidearm between swings, and raise a shield at any moment.

Your facing turns toward the mouse at a fixed speed, and attacks go where you face. You can walk backwards at full speed, but drawing a bow or a sling slows you down: plant your feet to shoot.

Hold a key to repeat a tool as soon as it is ready. A single press while it cools down does nothing; a double press queues one use. Hold Alt and press a keyboard slot to toggle auto-repeat. Traps, banners and thrown flasks aim at your cursor.

Damage = base × (1 + attack/100) × (1 − defence/100). Shields add defence for a few seconds; heals make you slower and easier to hurt while they work, so heal behind a shield, a wall or a friend. Everyone starts with 100 health; the Guard and Knight ranks add 12 each, and every Soma tier up to 30 adds 1 health and 1.5 attack. Out of combat for 8 seconds, you slowly heal.

Progress

Soma is permanent. Every Soma tier gives one key, and each key unlocks one tool in the Tools panel (K): Warrior, Hunter, Alchemist and Auxiliary paths, 70 tools to unlock plus 8 everyone has. Your toolbelt has 10 slots: 6 are open from the start and the others open at Soma tiers 3, 7, 13 and 21. Tier never adds health or damage. Change your toolbelt in Hanbin Haven or a castle your faction holds. A respec in the Haven returns every key for 5% of your soma.

Valor lasts one life: you lose all of it when you die or log off. It grows faster the more you have, and every kill gives more Soma the more Valor you carry, so staying alive is the grind and slaying a high-Valor enemy is the prize. NPCs give a fifth of what players give. Ranks give privileges: faction chat, troop commands, quick travel, renaming your faction, world chat (Ranks panel).

Your faction's territory sets the Valor cap (5,000 with no land) and adds a reward bonus.

Factions and conquest

Everyone is in a faction: one of three public ones, or a private faction of up to 10 that you found and share with a link (Factions panel).

A castle falls the moment only one faction is left inside its walls for 3 seconds: beat its defenders or lure them out. Gonfalons and Ensigns hold a castle while they stand; traps never do. Surviving defenders switch sides, and empty posts refill within a minute or so. After dying you rise in Hanbin Haven or at any fort your faction holds.

Orders: say follow, kill, scatter, hold, heal, obey or fend near your faction's troops (put a name first to order just one kind: rangers follow). Castle troops need the right rank; hired troops always obey you. rally my troops brings your hired troops to you after 10 seconds.

Quick travel (Marshall) from the map: 20 seconds standing still, taking 4× damage.

Wood and gold

Break log piles for wood; it pays for traps, deployed banners and bonfires. Gold comes from dungeon bosses, Pillagers and castle taxes; it hires troops and fortifies castles. Carried gold and wood are at risk: dying drops half. Gold banks itself in the Haven or your castles.

The isles

Hanbin, in the middle, holds the Haven and its gentle heartland. Timber bridges lead out to the other isles: the Frostmark to the north, Emberreach to the east, Mirewold to the west, the Sunscar to the south, and the small Gull Isles, Thornholm and Saltspire beyond them. The further an isle lies from Hanbin, the more dangerous it is. Bridges are narrow: hold one and you hold the way across.

Dungeons: cave mouths (purple rings on the map) lead to shared dungeons with a boss in the last room. Get out of the red circles.
