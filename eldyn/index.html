How to play✕

Move WASD · Aim with the mouse · Toolbelt: Left click Space Shift V Right click Middle click Q

K tools · F factions · M map · Tab ranks · Enter chat · [ ] turn · Esc close. Change any key under Keybinds (O).

Fighting

You fight with the 7 tools in your toolbelt: weapons, shields, maneuvers, heals, thrown concoctions, traps and banners. Every tool has a wind-up, a timed sequence and a cooldown. Tools in the same group share a cooldown (all weapons, all shields, all heals...), so one weapon swing and one shield at a time, but a weapon, a shield and a sidearm can all work together.

Your facing turns toward the mouse at a fixed speed, and attacks go where you face. You can back away while attacking forward, but backpedalling is slower.

Hold a key to repeat a tool as soon as it is ready. A single press while it cools down does nothing; a double press queues one use. Hold Alt and press a keyboard slot to toggle auto-repeat. Traps, banners and thrown flasks aim at your cursor.

Damage = base × (1 + attack/100) × (1 − defence/100). Shields add defence for a few seconds; heals make you slower and easier to hurt while they work, so heal behind a shield, a wall or a friend. Everyone has 80 health (Guard and Knight ranks add 10 each). Out of combat for 8 seconds, you slowly heal.

Progress

Soma is permanent. Every Soma tier gives one key, and each key unlocks one tool in the Tools panel (K): Warrior, Hunter, Alchemist and Auxiliary paths, 71 tools in all. Tier never adds health or damage, only choice. Change your toolbelt in Hanbin Haven or a castle your faction holds. A respec in the Haven returns every key for 5% of your soma.

Valor lasts one life: you lose all of it when you die or log off. It grows faster the more you have, and every kill gives more Soma the more Valor you carry, so staying alive is the grind and slaying a high-Valor enemy is the prize. NPCs give a fifth of what players give. Ranks give privileges: faction chat, troop commands, quick travel, renaming your faction, world chat (Ranks panel).

Your faction's territory sets the Valor cap (5,000 with no land) and adds a reward bonus.

Factions and conquest

Everyone is in a faction: one of three public ones, or a private faction of up to 10 that you found and share with a link (Factions panel).

A castle falls the moment only one faction is left inside its walls for 3 seconds: beat its defenders or lure them out. Gonfalons and Ensigns hold a castle while they stand; traps never do. Surviving defenders switch sides, and empty posts refill within a minute or so. After dying you rise in Hanbin Haven or at any fort your faction holds.

Orders: say follow, kill, scatter, hold, heal, obey or fend near your faction's troops (put a name first to order just one kind: rangers follow). Castle troops need the right rank; hired troops always obey you. rally my troops brings your hired troops to you after 10 seconds.

Quick travel (Marshall) from the map: 20 seconds standing still, taking 4× damage.

Wood and gold

Break log piles for wood; it pays for traps, deployed banners and bonfires. Gold comes from dungeon bosses, Pillagers and castle taxes; it hires troops and fortifies castles. Carried gold and wood are at risk: dying drops half. Gold banks itself in the Haven or your castles.

Dungeons: cave mouths (◉ on the map) lead to shared dungeons with a boss in the last room. Get out of the red circles.
