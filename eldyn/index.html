How to play✕

Move WASD · Aim with the mouse · Use skills 1–0, Left click = slot 1, Right click = slot 2 (hold to repeat)

K skills · F factions · M map · Tab leaderboard · Enter chat (/f message for faction chat) · Esc close

These are the default keys. Change any of them under Keybinds (O). You can reset your skill points for free in the Skill Trees panel, but only while standing in Hanbin Haven.

Soma is permanent and never capped. Every soma tier gives a skill point, every 4 tiers another relic slot (up to 6), and every 5 tiers another hotbar slot (up to 10). Each tier costs more soma than the last, so this is a long climb.

Relics (I) are your build. They change every attack you make: splash, lifesteal, pulling enemies in, burning, crits, chain lightning, exploding corpses and more. Stack relics that feed each other. They drop from elites (gold names), bosses and occasionally any monster, or come from crates sold in the Haven. Two of the same relic and level fuse into a stronger one.

Gold buys crates, pays for fusing and fortifies your faction's castles. Gold you are carrying is at risk: dying drops half of it for anyone to grab. It is banked automatically when you walk into Hanbin Haven or a castle your faction holds.

Dungeons: cave mouths (◉ on the map) lead to multi-room dungeons shared with everyone. Each ends in a boss with telegraphed attacks: get out of the red circles. Bosses drop the best relics and come back 8 minutes after they die.

Valor is capped and sets your rank, from Peasant to King. Your armour improves with rank. You lose 75% of your valor when you die.

Earn both by slaying monsters and players and by conquering castles. Danger (◆) rises toward the coasts, and so do the rewards.

Skill trees: Blade, Marksman, Alchemy & Sorcery, Command and Technique. Each tree branches, and some skills are passives. Skills have long cooldowns, so mix trees to build your own kit.

Factions: found or join one. Faction members and the soldiers of your faction's castles are your allies.

Conquest: every region has a fort, castle or moated citadel; the deadlier the region, the bigger the fortress. Castles pay taxes to their faction every minute, can be fortified with gold, and let you bank gold and swap relics inside. Enter its courtyard, defeat every defender (its guards and any players of the owning faction), then hold the courtyard to claim it. You need a faction. Hanbin Haven, the village at the centre, is a safe zone.

Parry a hit to stun and weaken the attacker, then Riposte for a double-damage lunge.

Rank rewards: higher ranks give more health and damage, and unlock Recall (Knight, B), travel between your faction's castles from the map (Baron), a damage aura for allies (Duke) and faster teleports (Prince). See the full list in the Ranks panel (Tab).
