How to play

Town and Mafia take turns. At night, roles act in secret. By day, everyone argues and votes someone out. Anyone can lie.

Each round

  1. Night. Public chat closes. Roles choose their targets.
  2. Morning. Any death is announced.
  3. Discussion. Accuse, defend, claim a role.
  4. Voting. One vote each, changeable until time runs out. Most votes is eliminated. A tie or no votes eliminates nobody.

Winning

  • Town wins when all Mafia are dead.
  • Mafia wins when living Mafia equal or outnumber everyone else alive.
  • Jester wins alone by being voted out. The game then goes on, and Town or Mafia can still win.

Roles

  • Mafia Know each other and chat at night. Together choose one non-Mafia player to kill each night.
  • Doctor Protect one living player each night, including yourself, but never the same player two nights running. You aren’t told if it worked.
  • Detective Investigate another player each night and privately learn “Mafia” or “Not Mafia”.
  • Villager No ability. Your reasoning and your vote are your weapons.
  • Jester Not on any team, and shows “Not Mafia”. Get voted out by day. Dying at night or surviving to the end is a loss.

Dead players watch and use a separate dead chat that the living can’t see. If you disconnect, reopen the page or the invite link and you’ll return to your seat.
