How It Works

1

The Psychic Gives a Clue

One player sees a secret target on a spectrum between two extremes (like “Bad Movie” to “Good Movie”) and gives a one-word clue.

2

Everyone Guesses

The other players discuss the clue and guess where the target is on the spectrum by dragging a dial or picking a number.

3

Score Points

The closer the guess is to the hidden target, the more points everyone earns. Spot-on guesses score maximum points!

Three Ways to Play

One Psychic (classic), One Guesser (reversed), and Teams (head-to-head). Works with 2-12 players.
