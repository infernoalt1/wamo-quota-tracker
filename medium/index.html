THE ART OF THINKING ALIKE

Two words.
One wavelength.

You and your partner see two words. Secretly enter one word that connects them. Once both answers are in, they reveal together.

Match on your first try for 3 points, second for 2, or third for 1. If you miss, your answers become your next two words. Three misses ends your round.

Capitalization and surrounding spaces are ignored; spelling must match exactly. Everyone earns their pair’s points. The host advances once all pairs finish.

With an odd number of players, one person sits out each round. Rotating mode shares sit-outs equally. Fixed mode keeps assigned partners together and rotates the sit-out through the final group of three.
