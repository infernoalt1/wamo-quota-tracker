THE 30-SECOND RULEBOOK

Think fast.
Stay in.

  1. One category, everyone in. Take turns naming something that fits. Your friends judge the answers.
  2. Beat the clock. Repeats and timeouts knock you out automatically.
  3. Last player standing wins a point. Everyone returns next round. Most points wins; ties share the crown.

Something doesn't fit? Call Out. After each answer, other players still alive have 2.5 seconds to call it out. With 3+ active players, one additional player must confirm within 4 seconds. The answerer and caller cannot confirm. Without confirmation, the answer counts.

With exactly 2 players still alive, the opponent's call-out succeeds immediately. The caller has 1.5 seconds to undo an accidental call-out, including after it succeeds. Eliminated players and spectators watch until the next round.

Memory mode hides each answer after its Call Out interaction during play. After the game, everyone can view all rounds and export the answers. The host can choose or invent a different category for every round in the lobby. Rhythm mode gives you a four-beat loop at 120 BPM: answer before your turn's beats run out. Sound is optional.
