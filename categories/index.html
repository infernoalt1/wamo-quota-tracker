THE 30-SECOND RULEBOOK

Think fast.
Stay in.

  1. One category, everyone in. Take turns naming something that fits.
  2. Beat the clock. A late, repeated, or invalid answer knocks you out for the round.
  3. Last player standing wins a point. Everyone returns next round. Most points wins; ties share the crown.

Questionable answer? Challenge it when challenges are enabled. Everyone except the answerer can vote, including spectators. A strict majority must reject it; ties count as valid.

Memory mode hides each answer after the review. Rhythm mode gives you a four-beat loop at 120 BPM: answer before your turn’s beats run out. Sound is optional.
