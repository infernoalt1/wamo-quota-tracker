01 / ANATOMY

The head that sets them apart.

The broad, flattened head is called a cephalofoil. Its shape varies between species. In scalloped hammerheads, the front edge has a central indentation and additional notches that give the head its distinctive scalloped outline.

OBSERVATION

A shark’s silhouette can be a useful identification clue. Look at the shape of the head and fins together.

02 / ECOLOGY

Life in a connected ocean.

Scalloped hammerheads inhabit tropical and warm temperate seas. Young sharks use shallow coastal habitats, while adults also occur offshore. Fishing pressure and bycatch are significant threats.

Tagging and population research help scientists understand where sharks travel and how their habitats connect.
